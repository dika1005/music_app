# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Project Name: MelodyFlow (Music Streaming App)

### 1. Vision & Overview
**Vision:** To provide a seamless, highly responsive, and visually stunning music streaming experience that adapts to any device, ensuring users can enjoy their favorite tracks without interruption.
**Overview:** MelodyFlow is a cross-platform mobile application built with Flutter. It focuses on high-performance audio playback, modern UI/UX (Material 3), and offline capabilities.

### 2. Target Audience
- **Primary:** Music enthusiasts, commuters, and students who need a reliable, fast, and visually appealing music player.
- **Secondary:** Audiophiles looking for features like equalizers and gapless playback.

### 3. Business & Product Goals
- **User Acquisition:** Achieve 10,000 active users within the first 3 months of launch via organic growth and App Store features.
- **Engagement:** Maintain an average daily session time of >25 minutes.
- **Performance:** Ensure app startup time is <1.5 seconds and audio buffering starts within <1 second.

### 4. Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **Crash-free rate:** > 99.5%.
- **Audio interruption rate:** < 1% (Background play and lockscreen controls must work flawlessly).
- **UI Responsiveness:** 60/120 FPS maintained during scrolling and animations.

### 5. Scope
#### Phase 1: MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
- User Authentication (Email, Google).
- Home/Discover Feed (Trending, New Releases).
- Search (Songs, Artists, Albums).
- Core Music Player (Play, pause, skip, seek, shuffle, repeat).
- Background Playback & Lockscreen Controls.
- Basic Library (Favorites, User Playlists).

#### Phase 2: Post-MVP & Advanced Features
- Offline Mode (Download & Encrypted Local Storage).
- Synced Lyrics.
- Audio Equalizer & Crossfade.
- Sleep Timer.
- Responsive Layouts (Tablet/Foldable support).
